Two ways to preserve a trace after experiencing Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia.

Not a test. Not a survey. A trace of reflection.

The Witness Report is a personal post-screening document. The participant chooses a quote or starting point, writes a few words and can download a light archival PDF with date, place and report number.

The document is generated locally in the browser. No data is sent or stored on a server.
