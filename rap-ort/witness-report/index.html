POST-SCREENING DOCUMENT

WITNESS REPORT AND RECORD OF PARTICIPATION

Two ways to preserve a trace after experiencing Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia.

The Record of Participation is available after selected screenings through a QR code. The Witness Report will be developed as a personal reflection document with quote selection, a short written trace and participant signature.

Why QR access?

The generator is not meant to be a public toy on the website. It is designed as part of the post-screening experience — available to participants who receive a link or QR code during the event.

This preserves the document’s special character: the participant does not only watch the work, but receives the possibility of downloading a commemorative Record of Participation with date, place and unique number.

Active

Record of Participation

An elegant black-and-gold commemorative document after the screening. The participant chooses one of three document characters: Archival Cinema, Museum Line or Ceremonial Frame.

The document is generated locally in the browser and prepared for printing as a personal keepsake of participation.

Witness Report

A future personal reflection mode: quote selection, a short written trace of what remains after the screening, participant signature and a light archival PDF document.

It will not be a knowledge test or evaluation form — only a personal record of the encounter with the work.

For the participant

If you received a QR code after the screening, open the generator and choose the character of the document. You may add your name, but you do not have to — the document may remain a general record of participation.

The document is generated locally. No data is sent or stored on a server.
