What This Guide Is

It is not a separate English version of the work. It is a guide — a way of understanding the structure, themes and moral questions of the Polish-language audiovisual experience.

The guide introduces testimony, conscience, memory, responsibility, silence and the pressure of history on human decision. It helps frame the experience without simplifying it.

Key Themes

Testimony

The work asks what it means to carry and transmit truth when silence becomes easier.

Conscience

The central tension is not only historical. It is also moral: what remains inside the person who must choose?

Silence

Silence functions as pressure, absence and space for reflection, not as emptiness.

Responsibility

The viewer is invited to consider what responsibility for truth can mean today.

How to Use the Guide

Before screening
Use it to introduce the form, historical seriousness and reflective character of the work without over-explaining the experience.
After screening
Use it to support discussion around testimony, pressure, memory, silence and human responsibility.
For international viewers
Use it as orientation for audiences who may not share the same historical or linguistic background.
For Polish diaspora audiences
Use it as a bridge between Polish-language memory and English-facing institutional context.
