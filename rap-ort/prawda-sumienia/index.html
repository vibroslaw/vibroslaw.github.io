RAP-ORT: PRAWDA SUMIENIA

A feature-length audiovisual work for institutional, cultural and educational screenings.

Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia is an approximately 65-minute audiovisual work inspired by the reports of rotmistrz Witold Pilecki and the moral question of testimony under totalitarian pressure. It combines narrative music, cinematic image, text and silence into one reflective screening format.

Screening / institutional enquiry Institutional use Polska wersja

What the work is

A serious screening format, not a promotional spectacle.

It is

An audiovisual reflection on testimony, conscience, silence and responsibility, built for attentive viewing in universities, museums, schools, cultural institutions and Polish diaspora contexts.

It is not

It is not a concert, not a classical documentary, not a fictional feature film, not a political campaign and not a sensational reconstruction of suffering.

Source-aware foundation

The page uses restraint because the subject requires it.

The work is inspired by Pilecki’s reports, the history of KL Auschwitz, prison testimony connected with the camp experience and later historical remembrance. Its language separates historical fact from artistic interpretation and avoids presenting viewer reflections as historical evidence.

The project does not attempt to replace scholarship, archival research or museum education. It is an authorial audiovisual form that can support reflection, discussion and memory work when placed in a responsible institutional context.

Historical anchor
Rotmistrz Witold Pilecki, his voluntary mission to Auschwitz, underground reporting and the moral burden of testimony.
Artistic form
Narrative music, cinematic visuals, written fragments and silence arranged as a single screening experience.
Ethical rule
No sensational language, no fake institutional endorsement, no claims beyond the source trail.

Screening format

Designed as one continuous experience.

Length

Approximately 65 minutes, depending on final event version and playback setup.

Structure

A sequence of narrative audiovisual chapters presented as one coherent work.

Language

Polish-language core work with English-facing institutional context and subtitles where prepared.

Atmosphere

Reflective, restrained, serious and suitable for commemorative or educational settings.

Institutional use cases

For contexts where memory, history and moral reflection need a careful form.

Universities

Screening with introduction or discussion around memory, testimony, totalitarian pressure and the responsibility of the individual.

Museums and cultural institutions

Reflective audiovisual event, anniversary programme or curated public-history context.

Schools

Educational screening or selected chapters with teacher-led preparation, sensitivity framing and discussion prompts.

Polish diaspora

A bridge between Polish historical memory and English-facing context for mixed audiences and younger generations abroad.

Commemorative events

A serious artistic element for remembrance dates connected with Pilecki, testimony, resistance and moral responsibility.

Discussion formats

Screening followed by Q&A, reflection material or a moderated conversation, online or in person where possible.

Previous presentation context

In May 2026, the project was presented in Oświęcim in connection with an academic and commemorative context at the Małopolska Uczelnia Państwowa im. rtm. Witolda Pileckiego. This context is referenced as part of the project’s development history, not as a substitute for formal institutional endorsement.

Future presentations can be adapted for universities, museums, schools, cultural centres and Polish diaspora organisations, with the level of introduction and discussion adjusted to the audience.

Responsible presentation

Content sensitivity

The work touches difficult historical and moral subjects. It should be introduced with calm context and not used as background entertainment.

Institutional wording

Event descriptions should avoid unsupported claims, inflated language and any suggestion of official patronage unless formally confirmed.

Full screening, selected chapters, screening with introduction, screening with Q&A, educational session or commemorative programme.
