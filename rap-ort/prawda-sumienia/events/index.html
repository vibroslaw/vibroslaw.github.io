28 January 2026

Brzeszcze — local and communal premiere

Event record / public post

Formatcommunity screeningAudiencelocal memoryMaterialsstage rhythm + violinNext usecultural centres

Context

The first public presentation took place in Brzeszcze, close to the memory landscape of the Oświęcim region. It marked the moment when the work moved from studio creation into a shared public space.

Adaptation

The screening gained a staged rhythm: children appeared between chapters and Bartosz Słatyński added guest violin parts, giving the projection moments of live breath and transition.

What this presentation showed

Brzeszcze proved that Rap-Ort can function not only as a projection, but as a communal event shaped by place, stage presence and local memory.

For future organizers

This format is especially useful for cultural centres, anniversary events and local memory programmes where the screening may be combined with simple live or ceremonial elements.

Materials used

Children between chapters, guest violin, staged transitions and local commemorative framing.

25 May 2026

MUP Oświęcim — commemorative and educational presentation

Institutional event record / public post

Formatinstitutional screeningAudienceacademic / commemorativeMaterialsWitness Report + Attendance RecordNext useschools / universities

Context

The presentation at the Małopolska State University named after Cavalry Captain Witold Pilecki took place on the 78th anniversary of Pilecki’s death, giving the screening a commemorative and academic frame.

Adaptation

This version used reflective support materials, including the Witness Report and Attendance Record, to help viewers move from the audiovisual work toward source-aware reflection.

What this presentation showed

The Oświęcim edition showed that Rap-Ort can work inside an institutional setting as a screening, but also as a starting point for discussion about testimony, responsibility and historical memory.

For future organizers

This format is suited to universities, museums, schools and remembrance institutions that need a serious but accessible audiovisual entry point into Pilecki, KL Auschwitz and moral responsibility.

Materials used

Witness Report, Attendance Record, interludes and source-aware reflection materials prepared for educational use.

21 June 2026

Sydney — planned first international presentation

Upcoming diaspora route / project record

FormatEnglish-subtitled screeningAudiencediaspora / internationalMaterialsintro video + EN subtitlesStatusplanned for 21 Jun 2026

Context

The Sydney presentation is prepared for Polish diaspora and international audiences, extending the project beyond Poland and the Polish-language context.

Adaptation

The screening is prepared with English subtitles and an introductory video that places the work in a wider context of memory, testimony and responsibility.

What this presentation is designed to show

The Australian route tests the project as an international, subtitle-supported memory experience for audiences who may not know the full Polish context of Pilecki’s story.

For future organizers

This route is relevant for diaspora communities, Polish cultural organizations abroad, universities and institutions looking for an English-subtitled screening format.

Materials used

English subtitles, introductory video and post-screening Report Room access prepared for diaspora and international viewers.
