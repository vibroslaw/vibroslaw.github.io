CHAPTER SYSTEM

A calm guide to the structure of Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia.

The work in chapters

A guide to the emotional and moral architecture of the screening.

Act I — Origins and choices

1. Lacrimosa Poloniae

An opening lament: Poland, loss, memory and the moral atmosphere into which the story enters.

2. Faith. Soil. Honour.

A first biographical and ethical orientation around duty, formation and responsibility.

3. The Young Ideologue

A parallel path shaped by ideology, ambition and the pressure of history on personal choices.

4. Operation Witold

The decision to enter danger is presented as a moral act, not as spectacle.

Act II — Inside the camp

5. An Army Under Stripes

Organisation, resistance and human dignity under extreme pressure.

6. Under the Same Number

A reflection on identity, memory and the way systems try to reduce people to numbers.

7. Interlude / Chapter Seven

A restrained bridge chapter around human relationships, pressure and moral contrast. Final public title to be confirmed.

8. Report

The testimony becomes a carried burden: truth leaving a place designed for silence.

Act III — Escape and transmission

9. Night of Escape

Escape is treated through tension, risk and responsibility for what must be carried out.

10. Beyond the Wires

The world outside the camp does not end the moral burden of testimony.

11. New Poland

The post-war order introduces new pressure, new language and new forms of silence.

12. Trial

A chapter of accusation, isolation and the collision between conscience and political machinery.

Act IV — Memory and responsibility

13. Pragmatist

A reflection on survival, power and the moral cost of adapting to systems.

14. Faith. Soil. Honour. — Reprise

The earlier values return changed by everything the viewer has witnessed.

15. Now We Are Free

An epilogue-facing reflection on memory after violence and silence.

16. Your Report

The final movement turns toward the viewer: what remains to be written, remembered and carried?

How institutions can use this page

Before or after a screening, not as a replacement for the work.

Before screening

Use it to explain that the work is structured as a continuous audiovisual experience made of narrative chapters.

After screening

Use it to return to selected chapters during discussion, reflection, programme notes or classroom prompts.
