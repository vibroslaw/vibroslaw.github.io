ANONYMOUS ARCHIVE

A quiet wall of traces.

The archive is intended only for anonymous reflections: no names, no surnames, no signatures and no personal data. A trace is not a review. It is what remains.

The archive does not ask for names, surnames or personal data. Every trace should remain anonymous. Do not include information that identifies you or anyone else.
