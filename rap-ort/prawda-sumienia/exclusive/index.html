WHAT THE WORK IS

A cinematic act of memory, grounded by testimony.

Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia is an audiovisual work about testimony, moral pressure and the record left by human choices under totalitarian systems.

It is not a documentary transcript and not a complete historical reconstruction. It combines confirmed historical basis with clearly marked artistic interpretation so the viewer can understand the sources, the compression and the question that remains.

01

Historical grounding

The work is anchored in reports, documented chronology and public historical sources connected with KL Auschwitz, wartime resistance and postwar repression.

02

Marked interpretation

Music, symbolic voices and cinematic compression deepen reflection. They are not presented as verbatim quotation unless explicitly marked.

03

Viewer responsibility

After the screening, the experience turns outward: what do you record, refuse, remember and carry forward?

JOURNEY MAP

A luminous report line across history.

The timeline is not a complete biography. It is an orientation line for the moral and historical pressure behind the work.

  1. The line begins with the life later bound to testimony, resistance and the Report.

  2. A second life enters the historical field, later shaped by imprisonment, politics and postwar power.

  3. The civic horizon behind duty, service and statehood becomes part of the work's memory field.

  4. The pressure system begins: occupation, violence, secrecy and choices made under force.

  5. The work turns toward the German Nazi concentration and extermination camp Auschwitz in occupied Poland.

  6. Resistance, observation and testimony become a moral record made inside extreme pressure.

  7. The report leaves the wire and enters the fragile space where testimony asks to be received.

  8. The postwar world does not end the moral pressure. It changes the uniform of power.

  9. After the screening, the record is not only historical. It returns as a question to the viewer.

FACTS VS INTERPRETATION

A curatorial frame for international viewers.

The page makes the distinction visible before the viewer reaches sources or participant actions.

I

Confirmed Historical Basis

The work is grounded in reports, institutional sources, documented chronology and recognized historical research.

II

Artistic Interpretation

Music, symbolic voices and cinematic compression are used to deepen reflection. They are not presented as verbatim historical quotation unless explicitly marked.

Nina is a symbolic figure of conscience and lost humanity. Symbolic scenes are used to ask a moral question, not to create new historical testimony.

III

Sources & Safeguards

Key claims are tied to public source IDs. The project avoids sensationalism, false endorsement and simplified historical claims.

Source grounding does not imply institutional approval, museum endorsement, university approval or official patronage.

Historical disclaimer

Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia is an audiovisual work inspired by historical sources, especially Captain Witold Pilecki's reports and documented histories connected with KL Auschwitz, wartime resistance and postwar communist repression. The project combines confirmed historical basis with clearly marked artistic interpretation.
