Veritas Humanum / SZTAB

SZTAB

A music-driven animated world of memory.

SZTAB is a world of projects devoted to people, events and decisions that cannot be reduced to a simple note. It combines music, narration and image, creating a distinct space of memory within Veritas Humanum.

Two Connected Directions

The SZTAB world develops in two connected directions: as the music album Raport z Pamięci and as an animated series of short historical forms.
