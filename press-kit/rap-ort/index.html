Institutional paragraph

“Designed for cultural, educational and institutional contexts, Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia presents sixteen narrative musical chapters as one continuous post-screening experience. The work does not attempt to replace historical study or archival material. It uses artistic form to return viewers to source-grounded questions of testimony, responsibility, memory and conscience.”

SOURCE AND INTERPRETATION

Responsible framing

This press kit describes an authorial historical-audiovisual work. The project is inspired by historical sources connected with Witold Pilecki, KL Auschwitz and postwar memory, while its music, visuals, narrative compression and symbolic voices remain artistic interpretation. Source links, public references and event contexts are provided for orientation and do not imply endorsement, patronage, curatorial approval or formal partnership unless explicitly stated.

Source boundary

Historical source and artistic interpretation are separated.

Visual boundary

AI-assisted or symbolic visuals are not archival evidence.

Restraint

The work avoids graphic sensationalism.

Dignity

Auschwitz memory is treated with restraint and dignity.

Institutional context

References to institutions or public events are contextual, not endorsement claims.

AUTHORIAL CONTEXT

Author

Piotr Lichwała / Vibrosław is the author of historical audiovisual works combining music, image, narrative structure and memory-based reflection. His projects explore Polish history, testimony, moral responsibility and the relationship between art and historical education. Rap-Ort: Prawda Sumienia is his central institutional work devoted to Witold Pilecki, the memory of KL Auschwitz and the question of conscience.
