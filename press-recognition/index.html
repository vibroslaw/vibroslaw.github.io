Media note

A short wording for articles, event pages and introductions.

Short description

Piotr Lichwała / Vibrosław is a Polish creator based in the United Kingdom, working across music, audiovisual storytelling, historical reflection and AI-assisted production. He is the author of Veritas Humanum, a connected universe of projects where memory, image, music and conscience meet.

Project description

Veritas Humanum is an authorial world of audiovisual projects developed around memory, music, image, testimony and responsibility. Its projects include Rap-Ort, SZTAB, Między Wierszami / Between the Lines and the musical works of Vibrosław.
