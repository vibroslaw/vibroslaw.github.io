AUTHORIAL PROFILE

Piotr Lichwała / Vibrosław creates authorial audiovisual projects.

His works operate between album, film, testimony, short-form visual storytelling and institutional experience. At their centre is the human being confronted with memory, choice, emotion and responsibility.

Contact

Creative Method

The creative process combines research, writing, music, image, editing and AI-assisted production tools. AI does not replace authorial intention — it becomes part of a workshop.

What remains essential is the subject, responsibility, language, restraint and the conscious guidance of the viewer through the experience.
