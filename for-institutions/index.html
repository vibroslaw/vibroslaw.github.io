FOR INSTITUTIONS

Screenings, educational licensing and reflective events.

This section is intended for schools, universities, museums, cultural centres, Polish diaspora organisations, programme curators and partners who wish to bring Veritas Humanum projects into an educational, cultural or commemorative context.

Send a screening or partnership enquiry

Recommended Formats

Flexible institutional use without losing the character of the work.

Audiovisual screening

A focused presentation of the work within an educational, cultural or commemorative context. Suitable for schools, universities, cultural centres and Polish diaspora events.

Screening with author introduction

A short contextual introduction before the screening, framing the work as an audiovisual experience of memory, testimony and responsibility.

Screening with Q&A

A screening followed by an online or in-person author conversation, usually 20–30 minutes, focused on memory, artistic process, history and viewer reflection.

Educational licensing

A time-limited non-theatrical licence for institutions wishing to use the work in an internal educational or commemorative setting.

Reflection materials

Participant-facing reflection prompts, discussion questions or a Witness Report pathway can support post-screening conversation without turning the work into a knowledge test.

Polish diaspora programme

A format designed for communities outside Poland, combining Polish-language cultural memory with English-facing context for mixed audiences.

Typical Event Flow

1. Introduction

Short institutional or authorial framing before the screening.

2. Screening

Presentation of the audiovisual work in the agreed format.

3. Reflection

Optional quiet moment, discussion prompt or Witness Report activity.

4. Q&A

Optional online or in-person conversation with the author.

What Institutions Receive

Depending on the agreed format, an institution may receive screening access, project description, event description, promotional copy, technical note, discussion questions, reflection materials and the possibility of an online or in-person author conversation.

The projects can be adapted to secondary schools, universities, museums, cultural institutions, commemorative events, educational programmes and Polish diaspora gatherings.

Core materials
Project description, screening information, suggested event wording and basic technical guidance.
Educational context
Reflection questions, optional worksheet structure and guidance for post-screening discussion.
Authorial presence
Optional short introduction or Q&A, online or in person depending on the event.
Technical needs
HD playback, projector or screen, reliable sound system and internet access only if online Q&A is included.

How to Enquire

When contacting, please include the institution name, city and country, proposed date, expected audience, preferred format, language needs and any additional context around the event.

Clear context helps prepare the right screening, Q&A, educational or diaspora-facing format.
