What Institutions Receive

Depending on the agreed format, an institution may receive screening access, project description, promotional materials, technical note, suggested event description, discussion questions, reflection sheet and the possibility of an online or in-person author conversation.

The projects can be adapted to different contexts: secondary schools, universities, museums, cultural institutions, commemorative events, educational programmes and Polish diaspora gatherings.
